Deliverable 4: Discussion
As N increases, the posterior covariance matrix S_N physically shrinks because the data contributes more information (beta * Phi^T Phi dominates), reducing the parameter uncertainty term phi(x)^T S_N phi(x). However, the irreducible observation-noise term (beta^-1) represents the inherent variance in the physical process generating the data, meaning predictions will always have a baseline uncertainty floor regardless of how accurately we estimate the polynomial weights.
