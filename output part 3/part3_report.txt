Deliverable 7: Discussion
In the coefficient path, the L1 penalty drives coefficients to ~0 one by one as lambda grows (0 are ~0 at lambda=1e-04, 4 at lambda*=0.10, 8 at lambda=1). The |w| penalty has a kink at 0, so its pull on w_j keeps magnitude lambda however small w_j gets, and w_j can sit at 0 whenever |Phi_j^T r| <= lambda (the L1 ball has corners on the axes); with a constant step the subgradient method only keeps it ~0, within about eta*lambda. The L2 pull, lambda*w_j, vanishes as w_j -> 0, so ridge only shrinks. At their best lambda, Lasso has 4 coefficients with |w_j|<0.01 versus 1 for Ridge, at similar ||w||_2 (1.47 vs 1.44) and lower test RMSE (0.0985 vs 0.1422). Past lambda*, train RMSE rises from 0.075 to 0.346 and test RMSE from 0.099 to 0.348; the two converge because the model is underfitting both sets.
