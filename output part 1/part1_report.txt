Deliverable 6: Discussion
In the loss-curve plot (eta=0.1), the SGD curve lies on top of Batch GD because Q is a square orthogonal matrix: its rows are orthonormal, so each single-row update changes only the component of alpha along that row and does not disturb the others. After one epoch every component has been multiplied by (1 - eta), exactly as in one Batch GD step, regardless of shuffle order. Mini-batch GD is slower because averaging divides the step by the batch size: the 9 samples in batches of 3 move with an effective step eta/3, so their components shrink by (1 - eta/3) per epoch. Only the singleton batch gets the full step, and the singleton is a random sample (1 in 10 per epoch), so the average log-rate is 0.9*ln(1-eta/3) + 0.1*ln(1-eta) = -0.041 versus ln(0.9) = -0.105 for Batch GD (about 0.96 vs 0.90 per epoch). This ~2.6x slower rate matches the plot, where Mini-batch flattens at about epoch 850 versus about 330. SGD and Mini-batch share the same per-epoch shuffle.
