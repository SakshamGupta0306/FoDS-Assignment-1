Deliverable 6: Discussion
In the loss-curve plot (eta=0.1), the SGD curve lies on top of the Batch GD curve because Q is a square 10x10 orthogonal matrix, so its rows are orthonormal: a single-row SGD update only changes the coordinate of alpha along that row and does not interact with the other rows, so after one epoch every coordinate has been multiplied by (1 - eta), exactly as in one Batch GD step (the shuffle order is irrelevant). Mini-batch GD converges more slowly because it averages the gradient: in each batch of 3 the effective step on those coordinates is eta/3, giving a per-epoch factor of about (1 - eta/3) instead of (1 - eta), and only the single leftover sample in the batch of size 1 receives the full step, which is why its line has a much shallower slope and flattens later (about epoch 850 versus about 330).
